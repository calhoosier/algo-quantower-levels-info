IVD Watch Publisher Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026.

IVD Watch Publisher is a personal monitoring utility operated by Amish Patel. This policy covers the monitoring uploader, not other applications.

Data and purpose

The utility uploads run timestamps, processing status, record counts, error summaries and sanitized diagnostic logs from the owner's local IVD Watch installation to the owner's private Google Drive. These records are used to identify failed or missed runs and troubleshoot the pipeline.

Google access

The utility requests Google's drive.file scope. Access is limited to files created by the app or explicitly authorized for use with it. It does not request unrestricted access to Google Drive or Gmail. OAuth credentials are stored locally for scheduled uploads and are not intentionally included in monitoring logs or this website.

Storage and disclosure

Monitoring records are stored on the owner's computer and in Google Drive. The owner may authorize connected tools to read these records for monitoring and troubleshooting. The publisher does not sell data, use it for advertising, or publish monitoring files on this website. Google processes stored data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and control

Monitoring records are retained until the owner deletes them or enables an explicit retention policy. The owner can revoke the app's access in Google Account settings and remove local credentials. Revoking access stops future authorized transfers but does not automatically delete previously stored records.

Limited use

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
